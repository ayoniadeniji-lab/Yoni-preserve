YONI PRESERVE — DEMO 01

WHAT THIS IS
A mobile-friendly, browser-based prototype for enhancing fashion illustration photos while preserving the original file. It offers Clear, Bold and Clean presets, effect strength, original/result preview, reset, and PNG export.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This is a local image-processing demo, not an AI restoration system. Clear and Bold adjust contrast; Clean applies mild smoothing and contrast. It does not understand artwork semantically, and no filter can guarantee that every pencil mark or tiny detail remains untouched. Always compare the preview with the original. The app does not upload your image to a server.

HOW TO TEST ON YOUR IPHONE
1. Open index.html in a browser after hosting it, or test it on a computer by opening index.html.
2. Tap Upload an illustration and choose an image from Photos/Files.
3. Choose Clear, Bold or Clean and adjust Effect strength.
4. Tap Export PNG to save/share the enhanced copy.

HOW TO PUBLISH WITH YOUR OWN GMAIL ACCOUNT (NETLIFY)
1. On a computer, open https://app.netlify.com/ and sign up/log in with your Google/Gmail account.
2. Keep the file named exactly index.html in the root of the folder.
3. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and drag the whole yoni-preserve-demo folder onto the drop area. If folder upload is not accepted, zip the folder and use the upload method offered on the page.
4. Netlify should provide a public demo URL after deployment. Open it on your iPhone to test.
5. To change the website later, edit index.html and deploy the updated folder again.

You do not need to share your Gmail password with anyone. Use your own account and choose a site name if prompted.
